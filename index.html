Monetiq Privacy Policy

App: Monetiq: Expense & Loan Tracker (com.wimosoft.monetiq) · Publisher: Wimosoft · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Summary

Monetiq is a personal expense and loan tracker. Everything you enter is stored on your device. Sign-up is optional and only needed for cloud backup. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Data you enter

Transactions, accounts, categories, people you lend to or borrow from (names and optional phone numbers), due dates, notes and screenshots you attach are saved in a local database on your phone. Wimosoft does not receive, collect or store this data.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you use Voice add. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on Android, usually Google's), and Monetiq reads the text on your device. Monetiq does not record or keep audio.
  • Notifications: to remind you about debts that are due. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Photos: only images you choose with the system photo picker are copied into the app's private storage. Monetiq cannot browse your gallery.

Sharing

Monetiq shares nothing on its own. When you tap “Remind on WhatsApp” or “Share PDF statement”, the app opens WhatsApp or the share sheet with a message or file that you review and send yourself.

AI assistant and voice

“Ask Monetiq”, Voice add and screenshot import work on your phone first. When a request can't be understood on the phone and the online AI service is available, only the text of that request (or the screenshot you shared, if the phone could not read it) is sent over HTTPS to our processing service and Google's Gemini API, together with a random install ID used for rate limiting. We do not store these requests. Google may use data sent to its free-tier Gemini API to improve its products. Nothing is saved to your records until you confirm it.

Optional account and cloud backup

If you choose to sign in (Settings → Account & backup), we use your email address to send you a one-time sign-in code. When you tap “Back up now”, a copy of your records (not receipt images) is stored in our database hosted by Supabase, protected so only your account can read it, and sent over HTTPS. You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and backups, email us at the address below.

Deleting your data

Data on your device is deleted permanently when you uninstall Monetiq. If you used cloud backup, email us to delete your account and backups; we do this within 30 days.

Children

Monetiq is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
