Delete your Monetiq account

App: Monetiq: Expense & IOU Tracker (com.wimosoft.monetiq) · Publisher: Wimosoft

How to request deletion

  1. Email asimglobal9@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Monetiq account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you signed in with, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account and reply to confirm within 30 days.

To delete only your cloud backups and keep the account, say so in the email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address linked to it
  • All cloud backups of your records (transactions, accounts, categories, people and loans)

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Data on your phone is not affected; uninstall the app to remove it from the phone.
